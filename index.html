Scoring your trait test

Conform helps your agent adapt to you, by using results from a short test that evaluates your personality. Read on to learn how it works or take the trait test to get started now!

How it works

The results of the trait test are separated into five factors, known as the "Big Five" markers for modeling personality. Each of them has a percentage associated with it based on the statements you ranked as way off, inaccurate, neither, accurate, or spot on. These values are used to generate a short description of your personality that's saved to a CONFORM.md file. Follow the instructions provided after you finish to use as intended.
